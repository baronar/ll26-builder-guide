4. 3D-Printed wire_lock Clip: Staggered +5V & GND Offset (22.2 mm)

Verified from wire_lock_1.stl (44.35mm × Ø11.36mm) & wire_lock_2.stl
✓ YES — Offsetting +5V (Red) and GND (Black) along the strip is 100% electrically safe and mechanically ideal!

• In your wire_lock_1.stl model (44.35 mm long × 11.36 mm outer diameter), the two side solder windows are intentionally staggered along the length of the sleeve: Window 1 sits at Y = 7.4–16.1 mm (center ~11.9 mm) and Window 2 sits at Y = 29.5–38.0 mm (center ~34.1 mm) — a 22.2 mm (~4 LEDs / 1 cut-pad) longitudinal offset.
• Why it works electrically: Both +5V and GND on your FCOB strip are continuous full-length copper busbars. A 22.2 mm (2.2 cm) offset between where +5V touches the strip and where GND touches the strip adds only ~0.004 Ω (4 milliohms) of copper trace, which is less than 0.02V even under heavy load — completely invisible!
• Why it is better mechanically: Staggering the two side windows by 22.2 mm keeps the 11.36 mm cylinder wall super strong (cutting both windows at the exact same Y position would slice through both sides of the tube at once) and prevents the Red and Black power joints from ever touching or shorting inside the narrow tube.
